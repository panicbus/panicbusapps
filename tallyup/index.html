How it works

Scan, confirm, redeem

Nothing to install, nothing for a customer to remember. Three steps, every time.

1Scan

One QR code by the register. A customer scans it on their own phone and types their number. Five seconds, nothing to install.

2Confirm

The check-in sits as pending until a staff member confirms it in person. Nobody can award themselves a point without a real interaction.

3Redeem

Set your own reward and how many visits it takes. Extra visits roll over past the threshold instead of being lost.

Why TallyUp
