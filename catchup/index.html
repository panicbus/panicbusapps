Streaks & The Pool

Bookmarks

Save a story for later. Read stories stick around in an archive for two weeks before they clear out.

Channels

Any topic on earth, organized your way

Add a channel for anything: a team, a band, a subject, a place. Subchannels narrow it further, like one player or one album, without losing the bigger picture.

Unlimited channels and subchannels, whatever you're trying to keep up with.

Reading

Preview first, click only when it's worth it

Cards with a snippet or image expand right in the app, so you can see what a story's actually about before deciding to read further. Cards without either link straight to the source.

AI filtering

An AI layer that filters, not decides

Turn on relevance filtering and Catch Up checks each incoming story against the channel or subchannel it landed in, dropping the ones that don't actually fit. It's optional, off by default, and runs on your own API key.

Signing in uses Google. Catch Up only asks for your basic account info, nothing more.

The Pool

An inbox you can actually empty

Work through a category and when you're through, Catch Up tells you. Nothing waiting to refill the moment you look away.
