How it works

Drop it, understand it, make it real

No folders, no tags, no filing. You write. Breakthru does the organizing.

1Drop it

A blank page. Type or dictate whatever is in your head, in any order. That is a thought drop.

2Understood

Each thought becomes a droplet in one of your channels, with a plain read on what it means.

3Make it real

Every droplet offers one next step. Nothing goes on your To do list until you tap Add.

Understood

Your words, then what they mean

Open any thought drop and it reads like a conversation with a friend who asks good questions. Your own words stay in italics, exactly as you wrote them. The AI never edits them.

  • What it means: a short, honest read on each thought.
  • Talk about this: a chat about one droplet, in place.
  • Harsh self-talk and worry loops are set aside, not thrown away.
  • It remembers. A new thought can connect to one from weeks ago.

The Board

Your whole life on one calm page

Every channel is a row: Work, Health, Money, Relationships, or whatever you name them. Each bar carries a mosaic of the days you wrote about it, so you can see where your attention has been going.

The front burner sits on top: up to three things that matter most right now, each with a reason and a next step.

mybreakthru.app
To do

A to do list you chose, one tap at a time

Breakthru suggests one step for a droplet. You decide whether it is worth doing. Add a date, tick it off, or open it to see the thought it came from.

  • Send any item to Todoist.
  • Due dates sync to their own Google Calendar.
  • Add your own items too, with no droplet needed.
mybreakthru.app/today
The Map

See what connects

Threads pools your droplets by channel and points out what it noticed: the thing that keeps coming back, the one conversation everything else is waiting on.

Tap a thread to see the thoughts behind it and one step you could take.

mybreakthru.app/map
Drift replay

Watch two weeks of thinking play back

Drift lays your thought drops out day by day. Press Replay and it tells the story: what connected to what, and which thread grew the most.
